## 7. Concrete unresolved issues from the latest read-only reviews

These are source findings, not newly reproduced on-car faults. Resolve them with focused reproductions and review before enabled deployment.

- **Active command arbitration:** `longcontrol.py` uses the scripted acceleration directly when `hook.active`; deeper normal demand wins only in `hook.handback`. Thus an active −0.5 request can replace a normal −2.0 request if the other gates pass. This is intentional open-loop ownership in the old design but an unresolved hazard for the requested repeated-use mode. Define safe arbitration/abort behavior and mark affected samples invalid for identification. Do not quietly change this into a different experiment or treat a naive `min()` as a validated response model.
- **Competing publisher:** setting the master True makes controlsd publish `alertDebug`, even before a trial is armed. `fullupdate.sh` and `tools/longitudinal_maneuvers/maneuversd.py` also publish it. Competing publishers can terminate the older publisher/controlsd and interrupt SCC12. Long-press arming does not fix this. Do not add another publisher or claim token consumption solves the collision.
- **Repeat selection:** raising `MAX_TRIALS` exposes −0.5, −1.0, −1.5, −2.0, −2.5 and later ramp/crossing profiles. A cancelled trial increments the index too. Repetition needs an explicit fixed-profile/sequence policy and clear labels, not just removal of the cap.
- **Gesture separation:** consume the whole long arming press, including its release. A held-at-start button must not arm. While ACTIVE, the first pressed frame already cancels; holding that same press must not subsequently rearm or start another trial.
- **Reset lifetime:** cruise-disengaged controlsd calls `LoC.reset()` repeatedly, and OFF/starting states also reset it. Disarming on every reset would erase parked arming immediately. Distinguish ordinary inactive updates from an actual fault/session end. Currently exceptions latch off, while ordinary faults do not establish the same permanent latch.
- **Mode coupling:** the current build reserves mappings and forces Standard across the entire enabled vehicle scope, not only while the hook is armed. Restoring ordinary actions outside armed mode would require cross-process coordination. Decide this explicitly; keep the change small.

The user discussed testing between ordinary drives on empty roads and said he can always brake/disengage. The preceding assistant declined activation under those conditions and offered offline fixes plus controlled testing. The long-press proposal improves deliberate activation but does not establish vehicle-response validation or sufficient space. No suitable measured closed test area is confirmed: earlier estimates were about 200 m then a turn, later only 50 m, then an approximately 100 m open-road proposal. Preserve this unresolved status; do not turn reassurance or this handover into a completed safety check.
